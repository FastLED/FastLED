1// layers.h - the three ring-native looks that MoodPainter mixes.
2//
3// Each layer renders the whole ring into its own float canvas every frame
4// and is blind to the others. The painter weights them by RoomSense::calm /
5// flow / groove, so a layer only has to look right at full strength.
6//
7// Nothing in a layer steps. Hits go through a Follower with a short attack
8// so even an instant event ramps over a frame or two, and every position is
9// advanced by rate * dt rather than snapped.

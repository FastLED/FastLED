void fl::PixelIterator::writeLPD6803Wide ( OutputIterator back_ins)

LPD6803 over a colour-managed channel's 16-bit drives, quantized once to the chip's 5 bits per channel (B3).

Same framing as encodeLPD6803: four zero bytes, 1rrrrrgggggbbbbb per LED in wire order, then n / 32 dwords of FF 00 00 00.

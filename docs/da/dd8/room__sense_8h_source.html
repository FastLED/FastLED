1// room_sense.h - what the room sounds like, as a handful of smooth signals.
2//
3// RoomListener polls the audio Processor once per frame and fills RoomSense.
4// Everything downstream reads RoomSense and nothing else, so the audio
5// pipeline can change without touching a single visual.
6//
7// There is no state enum. The three regimes (calm room, busy room, music
8// with a beat) are three weights that always sum to one. They move with
9// asymmetric attack and release, which gives the same "slow to enter
10// silence, fast to leave it" feel the old classifier needed timers for.

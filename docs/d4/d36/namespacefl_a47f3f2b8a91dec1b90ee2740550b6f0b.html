OKLab (s16.16) -> XYZ (s16.16).

The inverse of xyzToOklabQ16.

Not bit-exact in round trip: both directions round to nearest and the cube root truncates. oklabToXyzQ16(xyzToOklabQ16(v)) returns within 32 ULP over the working domain.

The other order degrades near black. OKLab cube-roots the cone responses, and that root's derivative, 1 / (3 * lms^(2/3)), diverges as a response approaches zero: around L = 0.05 it is roughly 1200, so any error in the response is hugely amplified coming back out. Restricted to in-gamut colours the worst measured round trip there is about 300 ULP. This is conditioning rather than representation – carrying lms or XYZ at Q32 was measured and does not fix it – and it costs the P7 mapper nothing. tests/fl/gfx/oklab_q16.cpp pins both ends.

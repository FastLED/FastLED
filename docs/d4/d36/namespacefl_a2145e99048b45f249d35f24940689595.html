One pixel: XYZ in s16.16 to five in-gamut drives – red, green, blue, white1, white2.

Same shape as the other two paths: one forward OKLab transform, one lightness comparison, then eight halvings, with every feasibility test going through the two-white allocation so the hull is the device's real one.

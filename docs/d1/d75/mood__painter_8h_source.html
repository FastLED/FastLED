1// mood_painter.h - mix the three layers, then trails and pulses.
2//
3// The painter owns the float scratch buffers and the post-process state.
4// It is the only thing that writes to the real LED array, and it does so
5// exactly once per frame, after every contribution has been summed in
6// float. That single quantisation is what keeps the output free of stair
7// steps.
